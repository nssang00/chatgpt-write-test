## What was actually wrong?

The previous corrective effort focused on topology (miter/butt joins and intersections), and its 8 topology unit tests passed. However, the RTX screenshot showed extensive jagged grey fragments and an unnatural green ground plateau. Real geometry analysis of the GLBs published by that failing screenshot run found *both*:

1. **Terrarium terrain triangle elevation jumps as large as 38m across an approximately 14m sample**, with maximum adjacent-sample grade of 1.86 (>100%), likely including urban elevation artifacts/aliasing. This is not reliable surveyed bare-earth road ground.
2. Roads were generated by sampling/interpolating that same extremely jagged terrain. Their unfiltered high LOD road-edge triangles spanned up to **21.76m in elevation** within one triangle and their road polygons exhibited tall cliffs.
3. Native Cesium3DTileset rendered this geometry correctly; simply changing the engine could not fix the upstream mesh. The previous 3D Tiles renderer did NOT report tile-load failures, but its screenshot was visually unacceptable.
4. The previous CI visual test failed on an uncategorized 404 console message. The smoke test now records actual HTTP failure URLs and ignores only verified missing favicon.ico (and not missing GLBs/JS).

## What changed

- Added `geobench/urban_terrain_repair.mjs`. **Visualization-only** approximation of ground based on a 9x9 neighborhood median, Gaussian sigma2 and a constrained adjacent slope projection capped at 25% per grid edge.
- The new filter is applied to original AWS/Mapzen Terrarium samples **before** creating OSM building heights or any road surfaces. The generated terrain, road elevations, buildings, and renderers now agree.
- For reproducibility, `GEO_TERRAIN_FILTER=urban` (default) vs `GEO_TERRAIN_FILTER=off` (baseline) explicitly controls the preprocessing.
- The render geometry of all four custom engines and Cesium's native GLB 3D Tiles shares these same corrected positions. The filter **must not** be applied to genuine NGII bare-earth DTM; data-product metadata will still report AWS/Mapzen source.
- Added synthetic roof-spike regression tests and full-scene individual triangle audits.
